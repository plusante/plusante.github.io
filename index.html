About

I am a graduate student at the University of Arizona since 2022, where I have worked with Dr. Eiichi Egami. I study galaxies and accreting supermassive black holes in the early universe, and I am a member of the JWST Advanced Deep Extragalactic Survey (JADES) collaboration. My work combines deep JWST imaging and spectroscopy with multiwavelength observations and forward modelling to investigate how black holes and their host galaxies grow together.

Research

  • High-redshift galaxies and AGN
    Tracing galaxy assembly and black-hole growth across cosmic time.
  • JWST imaging and spectroscopy
    Using NIRCam and NIRSpec observations to study faint, distant sources.
  • AGN–host decomposition
    Separating unresolved nuclear light from host-galaxy emission with PSF and Sérsic modelling.
  • Black-hole–galaxy coevolution
    Testing the connection between supermassive black holes and their host galaxies in the early universe.
